點擊之間，讓日文閱讀從「阻礙」變成「享受」！

還在為了看懂一個日文句子，在瀏覽器分頁與線上字典之間來回穿梭嗎？ Japanese Alchemy 是一款專為日文學習者設計的 AI 擴充功能。我們利用先進的 AI 技術，不只為你翻譯，更為你「解構」日文。無論是新聞閱讀、社群追蹤還是文件研讀，只需輕鬆選取文字，AI 魔法立即幫你點石成金。

✨ 核心強大功能
【瞬時解析】：選取段落或句子，點擊圖示立即獲得精準翻譯與深度語法分析。

【最強單字卡】：

標註重音：不再煩惱讀音高低，練就道地口音。
動詞分類：精確標示第一、二、三類動詞。
完整變化：一鍵呈現辭書形、て形、否定形等「日文完整動詞變化」。

【語法分析與例句】：不只給你意思，更解釋為什麼！提供對應 JLPT N1～N5 的實用例句。

【全漢字假名標註】：這是我們的堅持！所有內容皆附上【漢字假名】，徹底擺脫頻繁查字典的噩夢。

🚀 全新升級功能
Google 一鍵登入：跨裝置同步你的學習紀錄。

Save For Later (稍後閱讀)：遇到想複習的句子？一鍵儲存，隨時在 J-Buddy 網頁版深入溫習。

進階動詞變化庫：更新更全的動詞變體，幫助你應對更複雜的文本。

Turn Japanese Reading from a "Struggle" into a "Joy" with Just a Click!

Are you still switching back and forth between browser tabs and online dictionaries just to understand one Japanese sentence? Japanese Alchemy is an AI extension made just for Japanese learners. We use smart AI technology to not only translate but also "break down" Japanese for you. Whether you are reading news, scrolling social media, or studying documents, just select the text. Our AI magic works instantly to help you.

✨ Key Features
【Instant Analysis】: Select a paragraph or sentence, click the icon, and get an accurate translation and deep grammar breakdown right away.

【Best Flashcards】:

Pitch Accent: No more worrying about high or low sounds. Practice a natural accent.
Verb Types: Clearly marks Group 1, 2, and 3 verbs.
Full Forms: One click shows the Dictionary Form, Te-form, Negative form, and more.
【Grammar & Examples】: We don't just give you the meaning; we explain why! Includes practical examples for JLPT N1–N5 levels.

【Full Furigana Support】: This is our promise! All content comes with Furigana, so you can stop looking up words in dictionaries all the time.

🚀 New Upgrades

Google One-Tap Login: Sync your learning history across devices.
Save For Later: See a sentence you want to review? Save it with one click and study it later on the J-Buddy website.
Advanced Verb Library: Updated with more verb forms to help you with harder texts.
Better AI Model: More accurate and complete answers.
